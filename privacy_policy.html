MotoTrack — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026 · Arela, Barangay Pob. 3, Nagcarlan, Laguna

MotoTrack is the official mobile app of one shop: our motorcycle parts and service store in Nagcarlan, Laguna. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We wrote this in plain language because you deserve to understand what happens to your data. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to it.

1. What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and (if you choose to add them) your mobile number, motor model, and plate number.
  • Orders and bookings — parts you reserve, services you book, chats you send us, and GCash payment reference details needed to confirm your downpayment.
  • Device push token — so staff updates ("your part is ready!", "your receipt was approved") can reach your phone as notifications.
  • Staff & owner — the owner may enter customer name, contact number, plate, and motor model themselves for walk-in jobs.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and verify your account.
  • To track your reservations, service bookings, and payments.
  • To notify you about your orders, receipts, and account security.
  • To let you message staff about your booking.
  • To show honest shop reports (sales, inventory) to the owner only — customer identities are not shown to other customers.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your information. To anyone. Ever.
  • We never share your details with advertisers.
  • We never require your phone number or plate number to sign up — those fields are optional and you can add them later.
  • We never ask for your GCash password or full bank details. Payments are made directly through GCash; the app only records the shop copy of the payment amount and reference.

4. Where your data lives

Account data is stored on Firebase (Google's secure infrastructure) and our Supabase project. Both encrypt data in transit and at rest. Password-reset and verification emails are sent only to the exact email address you registered — never to anyone else's inbox. Access to shop data is controlled by roles: customers see only their own jobs, staff see only work orders, and the owner sees reports.

5. Your rights (RA 10173, Data Privacy Act of 2012)

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct anything that's wrong or outdated;
  • demand deletion of your data ("right to erasure");
  • object to processing and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

You can update your own info in Profile → Update Info. See Section 5.1 for the full account-deletion steps.

5.1 How to delete your account and data (MotoTrack)

You may delete your MotoTrack account and the personal data connected to it at any time. Two ways:

  • Email the shop owner at mototrack.owner@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my MotoTrack account". Send it from the same email address you used to register, and include your full name as registered. For your own security you don't need to send your password.
  • Visit the shop in person (Arela, Barangay Pob. 3, Nagcarlan, Laguna) and tell any staff member you want your account deleted. Bring a valid ID matching your registration.

What gets deleted: your account login (Firebase Authentication record), your profile details (name, email, mobile number, motor model, plate number, username), your device push-notification tokens, and your Rider Rewards card. Your chat messages and booking records linked to your account are also removed.

What we keep (and for how long): completed work orders, reservations, and sales records are retained in the shop's books for record-keeping right — required of Philippine businesses by the BIR — but with your personal details (name, contact, account link) removed, so the records no longer point to you as a person. There is no extra retention period for your profile itself: it is deleted when we process your request (usually within 3 business days, at most 30).

There is no fee for deletion, and deletion never affects accepted bookings that are already in progress — staff will finish your current job first.

6. Retention

We keep your account data while your account exists. Completed transactions are kept for shop record-keeping (sales history), but a deleted account's personal details are removed (see Section 5.1). Password reset links expire after 1 hour.

7. Safety tips inside the app

Please keep your log-in private. If someone else uses your account, they could see your bookings and chat history. Notify us immediately at the email below if you suspect this.

8. Contact

Questions or requests? Email mototrack.owner@gmail.com or visit the shop at Arela, Barangay Pob. 3, Nagcarlan, Laguna.

MotoTrack — Terms of Service

  • The app serves one shop only — our Nagcarlan store. Reservations and bookings are requests until staff or the owner confirms them.
  • Prices are shown in pesos. Part prices may change; the price confirmed by staff is the price you pay.
  • A GCash downpayment (half the total for stock orders) reserves your part. Keep your GCash receipt — you upload or show the reference number to confirm the payment.
  • Cancelled or unclaimed reservations are handled per shop policy; the downpayment may be credited to a future order rather than refunded in cash.
  • Repairs are performed at the shop. We will tell you upfront if a job is beyond our tools or parts.
  • Be kind in chat. Abusive messages or fake bookings can get an account suspended.
  • We may update this policy as features grow. Big changes will be announced in the app; the effective date at the top always tells you the current version.

You are in control of your data. Wherever the app requires an email, it is your own — you can always contact us to delete everything.
