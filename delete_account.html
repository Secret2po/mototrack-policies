Delete Your MotoTrack Account & Data

MotoTrack — the official mobile app of the MotoTrack motorcycle parts & service shop, Arela, Barangay Pob. 3, Nagcarlan, Laguna.

You can delete your MotoTrack account and the personal data connected to it at any time. There is no fee, and you do not need to give a reason.

Steps to request account deletion

  1. Email the shop owner at mototrack.owner@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my MotoTrack account". Send the email from the same email address you registered with and include the full name on the account. For your security, never send your password.
  2. Or visit the shop in person (Arela, Barangay Pob. 3, Nagcarlan, Laguna) and tell any staff member you want your account deleted. Bring a valid ID that matches your registration.
  3. That's it. We delete the account ourselves — you don't need to uninstall anything afterwards, although uninstalling the app is always your choice.

What data gets deleted

  • Your account login (Firebase Authentication record).
  • Your profile details: name, email address, mobile number, motor model, plate number, and username.
  • Your device's push-notification tokens.
  • Your Rider Rewards (loyalty) card.
  • Your chat messages and the booking records linked to your account.

What data is kept (and why)

Completed work orders, reservations, and sales records stay in the shop's books for record-keeping duties required of Philippine businesses. These records are retained with your personal details removed, so they no longer link back to you as a person.

How long it takes

The request is processed usually within 3 business days, at most 30 days. Deletion never affects a booking already in progress — staff will first finish your current job.

Deleting your data without deleting your account

MotoTrack does not currently offer a way to delete part of your data while keeping your account. If you only want a piece of information corrected or removed, contact us anyway — simple corrections can be made from your Profile screen in the app, and other requests are handled by the shop personally.

Questions

Email mototrack.owner@gmail.com. You can also read our full privacy policy at secret2po.github.io/mototrack-policies/privacy_policy.html, and exercise any rights you have under RA 10173 (the Data Privacy Act of 2012), including filing a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
